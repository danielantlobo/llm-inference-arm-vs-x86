Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that involves training algorithms to make predictions or decisions based on data. It uses statistical models to analyze and learn from data, allowing it to improve its performance over time. Deep learning, on the other hand, is a specific type of machine learning that involves the use of neural networks to analyze and interpret data. Neural networks are composed of layers of interconnected nodes or "neurons" that process and transmit information. Unlike traditional machine learning models, deep learning models are able to automatically learn and improve their performance without the need for extensive manual tuning. This is due to the complex architecture of neural networks, which allows them to learn and represent complex relationships between data.
