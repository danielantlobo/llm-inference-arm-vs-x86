Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that involves training algorithms to make predictions or take actions based on data. Deep learning is a subfield of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to analyze complex data.
Machine learning is a type of AI that enables systems to automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed. It involves training algorithms on data to make predictions or take actions. Machine learning can be applied to a wide range of tasks, including image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and decision-making. An example of machine learning is a self-driving car system that uses data from sensors and cameras to navigate roads and avoid obstacles.
Deep learning is a subfield of machine learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers to analyze complex data. It is particularly effective for image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and other tasks that involve pattern recognition. Deep learning models are trained on large amounts of data and can learn complex patterns and relationships that are difficult for humans to identify. An example of deep learning is a facial recognition system that uses a convolutional neural network (CNN) to identify individuals based on their facial features.
