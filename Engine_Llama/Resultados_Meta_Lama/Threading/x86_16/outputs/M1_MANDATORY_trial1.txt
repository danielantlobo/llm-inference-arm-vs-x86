Machine learning and deep learning are two related but distinct concepts in the field of artificial intelligence (AI). 
Machine learning is a subset of AI that involves training algorithms to make predictions or decisions based on data. 
The key characteristics of machine learning are: 
- It involves training algorithms on data to make predictions or decisions. 
- It can be used for classification, regression, clustering, and other tasks. 
- It often involves feature engineering and data preprocessing. 
- It can be applied to a wide range of domains, including image recognition, natural language processing, and time series forecasting. 
Deep learning, on the other hand, is a subset of machine learning that involves the use of neural networks with multiple layers to analyze data. 
The key characteristics of deep learning are: 
- It involves the use of neural networks with multiple layers to analyze data. 
- It is often used for image and speech recognition tasks. 
- It requires large amounts of data and computational resources to train. 
- It can learn complex patterns and relationships in data.
