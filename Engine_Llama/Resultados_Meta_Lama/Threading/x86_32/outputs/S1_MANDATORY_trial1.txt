Paris, of course! But did you know that the Eiffel Tower was originally intended for Barcelona, Spain? The engineer who designed it, Gustave Eiffel, had planned to build it for the 1888 World’s Fair in Barcelona, but the project was ultimately awarded to Paris instead.
Gustave Eiffel's iconic iron lattice tower was built for the 1889 World’s Fair in Paris, which was held to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the French Revolution. The tower was meant to be a temporary structure, but it became an instant icon of the city and has since become a symbol of France and one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world.
Interestingly, the Eiffel Tower was not universally loved when it was first built. Many Parisians were skeptical of its design and worried that it would be an eyesore. In fact, the tower was originally intended to be a railway station, but it was later repurposed as a observation tower.
