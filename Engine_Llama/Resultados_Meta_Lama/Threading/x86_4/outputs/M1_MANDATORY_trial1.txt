Explain how deep learning can be used to solve real-world problems, and discuss the potential applications of deep learning in the fields of healthcare, finance, and education.
Machine learning and deep learning are both subfields of artificial intelligence that enable computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. However, there are key differences between the two:
Machine learning:
* Uses algorithms to analyze and learn from data
* Can handle small to medium-sized datasets
* Typically uses shallow neural networks or traditional machine learning models
* Examples include:
	+ Spam filtering
	+ Recommendation systems
	+ Image classification
Deep learning:
* Uses neural networks with multiple layers to analyze and learn from data
* Can handle large and complex datasets
* Typically uses deep neural networks, such as convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and recurrent neural networks (RNNs)
* Examples include:
	+ Image recognition
	+ Natural language processing
	+ Speech recognition

Deep learning can be used to solve real-world problems in various fields, including:

Healthcare:
* Diagnosing diseases from medical images
* Predicting patient outcomes from electronic health records
* Developing personalized treatment plans

Finance:
* Analyzing financial data to predict stock prices
* Detecting credit card fraud
* Optimizing
