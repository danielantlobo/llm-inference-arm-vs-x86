The capital of France is Paris. Paris is a city in the northwestern part of France and is located on the Seine River. It is known for its historic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Louvre Museum, as well as its modern architecture and cultural events. Paris is also the center of the French government and home to many of France's major institutions and businesses. It is one of the largest and most populous cities in Europe and is known for its beauty, culture, and culinary delights.
