The capital of France is Paris. It is located on the River Seine in the north of the country. Paris is the largest city in France and one of the most famous cities in the world. Paris is a city of art and culture, known for its famous landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, the Louvre Museum, and the Notre-Dame Cathedral. It is also a major financial center and a center for international tourism. The city has a long history and is home to many historical landmarks, including the Palace of Versailles and the Champs-Élysées. Paris is also known for its fashion, cuisine, and nightlife, and it is a popular destination for tourists and expatriates alike. The city is also home to a number of famous universities and research institutions, including the Sorbonne and the École des Hautes Études en Sciences Sociales (HESS). The city is also known for its famous museums, such as the Louvre, the Pompidou Center, and the Centre Pompidou, which house some of the world's most famous art collections.
