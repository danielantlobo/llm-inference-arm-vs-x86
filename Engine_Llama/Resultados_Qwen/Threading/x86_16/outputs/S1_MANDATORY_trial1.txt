The capital of France is Paris. Paris is a city located in northern France and is known for its history, culture, and art. It is also the largest city in the country and is home to many famous landmarks, including the Eiffel Tower and the Louvre Museum. Paris is also a major economic and cultural center, and it is home to many important institutions, including the headquarters of many multinational corporations and the headquarters of the European Union. The city is also known for its fashion, art, and cuisine, and it is a popular tourist destination for visitors from around the world.
