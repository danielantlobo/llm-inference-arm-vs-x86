Paris. Paris is the capital city of France. It is the most populous city in France and the second most populous in Europe. It is located on the River Seine in northwestern France. It is the most visited city in the world with over 25 million visitors annually. It was founded by the Roman Empire and was the capital of the Kingdom of France from 987 until 1800. The city of Paris is a major global city and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Paris is renowned for its cultural and artistic heritage, architecture, fashion, gastronomy, and nightlife. The city has more than 130 museums, 1800 bridges, 2100 parks, 2000 cafes, 1800 restaurants, 2000 boutiques, 2000 hotels, 2000 banks, 2000 bookstores, 2000 theaters, and 2000 churches.
