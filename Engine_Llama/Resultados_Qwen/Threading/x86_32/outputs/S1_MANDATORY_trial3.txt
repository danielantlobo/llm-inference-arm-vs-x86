The capital of France is Paris. It is located on the Seine River in the north of the country. Paris is a major city in both France and the wider European Union. It has a population of around 2 million and is the most populous city in France and the second most populous city in the European Union. Paris is famous for its famous landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, Louvre Museum, and Arc de Triomphe. Paris is also known for its art, fashion, and culture. The city is home to many museums, theaters, and other cultural institutions. Paris is also known for its food, with its famous cuisine including baguettes, croissants, and cheese. Paris is also known for its nightlife, with many bars, clubs, and restaurants open late into the night. Paris is also a popular tourist destination, with millions of visitors coming to the city each year to see its landmarks and enjoy its culture.
