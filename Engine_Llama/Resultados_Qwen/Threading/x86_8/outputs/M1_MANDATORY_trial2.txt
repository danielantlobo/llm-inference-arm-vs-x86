Machine learning and deep learning are two related fields in the broader field of artificial intelligence. While they share many similarities, there are some key differences between them.
Machine learning is a subset of AI that focuses on the development of algorithms and statistical models that enable computers to learn from data and make predictions or decisions based on that data. It involves training models using labeled data, where the model is given a set of inputs and their corresponding outputs, and then the model is trained to predict the output for new inputs. Machine learning can be supervised, where the model is trained on labeled data, or unsupervised, where the model is trained on unlabeled data.
Deep learning is a subfield of machine learning that focuses on the development of neural network models that can learn from complex patterns and structures in data. These neural networks are made up of many layers, each of which performs a different function, and can learn to recognize patterns in data through a process called backpropagation. Deep learning models are often used for tasks such as image and speech recognition, natural language processing, and game playing.
