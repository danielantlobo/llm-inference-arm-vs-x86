Quantisation can help address these challenges in several ways:

- **Reducing Model Size**: Quantising the model can reduce its memory footprint, allowing it to fit within the memory constraints of CPU-only systems.

- **Faster Weights Loading**: Quantised models typically require fewer operations to compute, which can lead to faster weight loading during the decoding phase.

- **Improved Inference Speed**: Quantisation can also improve inference speed by reducing the number of computations required to generate predictions.

By applying quantisation techniques to transformer models, researchers and developers can overcome the memory constraints and slow inference speeds associated with CPU-only systems, making it more feasible to deploy large transformer models in resource-constrained environments.
