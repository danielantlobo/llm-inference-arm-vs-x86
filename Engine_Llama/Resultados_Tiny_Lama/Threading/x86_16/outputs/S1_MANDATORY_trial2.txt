1. Paris

II. France's National Symbols

1. The Eagle - Representing the sky, strength, and freedom.

2. The Lion - Representing the courage, strength, and pride.

3. The Sun - Representing the warmth, light, and renewal.

4. The Cross - Representing the faith, sacrifice, and piety.

5. The Chestnut - Representing the fertility, health, and agriculture.

II. France's National Emblems

1. The Fleur-de-Lys - Representing the royalty, the Crown, and the royal family.

2. The Lion Rampant - Representing the royalty, the Crown, and the royal family.

3. The Sun Rising - Representing the royalty, the Crown, and the royal family.

4. The Chestnut Raised - Representing the royalty, the Crown, and the royal family.

5. The Sun and Chestnut - Representing the royalty, the Crown, and the royal family.
